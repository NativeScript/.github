# NativeScript Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA)

- **Project Name:** NativeScript (OpenJS Foundation)
- **Project Entity:** NativeScript (OpenJS Foundation)
- **Contact:** [operations@openjsf.org](mailto:operations@openjsf.org)

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement path is for legal entities or employers that own intellectual property being contributed to NativeScript. Individuals contributing work they own personally (or with employer waiver/permission under Section 4 of the ICLA) should sign the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](./individual-cla.md) directly on their pull request instead.

## How to Execute a Corporate CLA

1. Have an authorized representative of your organization contact [operations@openjsf.org](mailto:operations@openjsf.org) referencing:
   - **Project Name:** `NativeScript (OpenJS Foundation)`
   - **Project Entity:** `NativeScript (OpenJS Foundation)`
   - Organization legal name and address
   - Authorized representative name, title, and contact email
   - GitHub usernames authorized to contribute on behalf of the organization
2. Once the Corporate CLA is accepted by the OpenJS Foundation / NativeScript Technical Steering Committee (TSC), a maintainer records the authorized GitHub usernames in [`cla-signers.json`](https://github.com/NativeScript/.github/blob/cla-signatures/cla-signers.json) on the `cla-signatures` branch with `"cla": "corporate"`.
3. Comment on your open pull request letting maintainers know once your Corporate CLA has been submitted so the `✍️ CLA` check can be re-run.

See the [NativeScript CLA Guide](../../CLA.md) for full details.
